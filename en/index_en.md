# R-layer Mode Theory (RLMT)
An independent research program in theoretical cosmology by Tsuyoshi Tohi.
Since July 2026 its predictions are tested against cosmological data, and every
result is reported, including negative results and corrections.

---

## 🔷 Current status (October 2026)
Since July 2026 the theory is tested against data. The 2026 papers fit RLMT's
predictions to Planck, ACT, DESI and Pantheon+ with public likelihoods, and
report negative results and corrections in full. Where they conflict with an
earlier Volume, the 2026 papers take precedence (see *Archive* below).

**What the data say so far**
- **Primordial spectrum (RLMT-lite).** Planck 2018 alone: Δχ² = +0.9, ΔAIC = +10.9.
  ACT DR6 with Planck (P-ACT-lite): Δχ² = −4.4, ΔAIC = +5.6. ΛCDM is preferred
  on grounds of economy; the data neither exclude nor require the three-mode mixture.
- **Non-Gaussianity.** f_NL = 0 in both readings of the mixture; not testable at present.
- **Tensors.** RLMT does not predict r at present (the direct horizon-exit reading was rejected).
- **Hubble tension.** Late-time Ω-layer dark-energy models fit as well as w0wa but leave
  H0 at about 68–69.5 km/s/Mpc; most of the gain comes from the shape
  "w = −1 today, phantom in the past", not from the layer structure.
- **Early modified gravity.** The conformally coupled Layer-2 scalar is exactly dark radiation (no-go).
- **Shortcuts through the R-direction.** Constrained by GW170817 to be of no practical use.

## 🗄 Archive (theory-only Volumes, March–July 2026)
RLMT was first written down on March 16, 2026. The Volumes from that period are
kept as the record of how the theory developed. They are theory-only and were
not fitted to data. Claims in them that the papers from July 2026 onward
(titled "RLMT: ...") test and replace include the H0 triplet of Vol. XLI and
the layer-dependent expansion rates of Vol. XLI-L.
