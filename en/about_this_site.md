## About This Site

This site presents R-layer Mode Theory (RLMT), an independent research program
by Tsuyoshi Tohi, in English and Japanese.

- **Current status and papers.** Since July 2026 RLMT's predictions are fitted
  to cosmological data (Planck, ACT, DESI, Pantheon+) with public likelihoods.
  The papers titled "RLMT: ..." report all results, including negative results
  and corrections, with code attached to each Zenodo record.
  → [RLMT home](index_en.md)
- **Archive.** The theory-only Volumes written from March to July 2026 are
  kept as the record of how RLMT developed. Where they conflict with the
  papers, the papers take precedence. → [Volume list](volumes.md)

**R-layer Mode Theory (RLMT)** — a unified framework that connects

- the internal mode structure (AUP / MUP / CUP)

- the full RLMT theory in both English

- appendices (X, Y, W)
